GitHub Repository ↗ Explore System Design
Project objective Make fitness tracking more practical for hostel students by using fixed mess menus as the source for meal tracking and combining nutrition with workout information.

About HostelFit

A single platform for nutrition and fitness management in the hostel environment.

Hostel students often have limited control over their meals because hostel mess menus are fixed in advance. HostelFit reduces the effort of manual food searching by providing pre-loaded mess menus and combines meal and activity data to show an approximate daily calorie balance.
